# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Research labs, benchmark owners, and legal-agent reviewers use the trace explorer on desktop monitors during model evaluation and incident review. They need to compare many runs, understand recurring behavior, and inspect one action sequence without reading raw logs.

## Product Purpose

Clavia Trace Index turns agent run records into immutable evidence. Success means a reviewer can explain what a group of trajectories did, compare outcomes across models, and move from an aggregate result to the exact tool call or model step that supports it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and warm. The interface should feel like a reliable research instrument with Flamecast's quiet confidence and ledger-like density.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic SaaS dashboard, a neon observability console, an editorial marketing page, and a forensic log viewer that exposes every payload at once. Avoid hero-metric templates, identical card grids, glass effects, decorative gradients, and color that has no state meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Make the evidence primary. Every aggregate result must lead to the traces that support it.
2. Reveal detail on demand. Show action shape first and keep reasoning or large payloads collapsed.
3. Use density with discipline. Compact rows and fixed rails should speed comparison without reducing legibility.
4. Preserve review context. Dataset identity, redaction policy, outcome, and trace position must stay visible.
5. Spend emphasis on state. Accent color, motion, and elevation must explain selection, outcome, or action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, focus, semantics, and keyboard use. Do not rely on color alone for status. Support reduced motion, 200 percent text zoom, narrow screens, and both light and dark themes.
